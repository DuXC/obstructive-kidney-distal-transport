# Discovery cohort annotation — 5 October 2026

The patient recruitment Methods of Reck et al. (Nature Communications 2025;16:4745; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59997-4) describe six renal cell carcinomas and one oncocytoma in the unobstructed reference group, alongside five obstructed upper tract urothelial carcinomas. Supplementary Data 2 supplies 12 donor profiles but does not link the individual control IDs to histology.

The source Results use a seven-RCC description. This annotation follows the more specific recruitment Methods for the aggregate composition and uses “unobstructed renal tumour reference” for group labels. Individual histology is recorded as unlinked. The donor set, group assignments, count matrices, eligibility, gene/program definitions and statistical estimates are unchanged.

The timestamped analysis plan retains its original bytes and is read together with this dated annotation. Current labels, derived metadata and their input hashes are synchronized by the accompanying patch. Source Data 1 retains all numerical cells and has updated Guide metadata. The manuscript reports the recruitment description with source attribution.
